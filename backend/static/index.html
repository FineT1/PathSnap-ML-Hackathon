Why Random Forest is the Champion Model

1. Top-1 Accuracy: Outperformed all candidate models on the holdout student-test set with 75.35% point-level candidate accuracy.
2. Log Loss Calibration: Achieved the lowest cross-entropy loss (0.6167), critical because these probabilities directly serve as HMM emission likelihoods.
3. Ensemble Regularization: Random subsampling (`max_samples=0.2`) and tree depth limits (`max_depth=12`) effectively prevent overfitting to specific trips.

Why Naive Bayes Was Not Selected

While Gaussian Naive Bayes achieves reasonable Top-1 rankings (74.93%), its strict conditional feature independence assumption produces highly uncalibrated probability peaks, resulting in a very poor log loss of 0.6909. This distorted confidence degrades HMM Viterbi decoding in ambiguous multi-lane corridors.

Evaluation Methodologies: Strict Boundary Rules

• Zero Data Leakage: The reference `.route` files are held strictly out-of-band and are NEVER fed into the feature engineering, ML prediction, emission scoring, or Viterbi algorithm.

• Continuity vs. Accuracy: An HMM can achieve 100% route continuity by finding a legal continuous path, while point accuracy measures exact match against noisy GPS fixes.
